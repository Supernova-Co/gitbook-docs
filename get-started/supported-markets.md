# Supported Markets

Trade on [Rates Exchange](https://beta.rates.exchange).

A market is defined by its underlying rate and expiry. Check both when choosing a market.

## Available markets

| Protocol | Underlying rate | Network | Expiration schedule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aave | USDC borrow rate | Ethereum mainnet | Monthly |
| Aave | USDT borrow rate | Ethereum mainnet | Monthly |

## Market schedule

- **Expires:** Last Friday of the month at 15:00 UTC.
- **Opens:** Approximately two weeks before the expiration month begins.

## Before trading

The network above identifies the underlying rate source. For the network and assets used to fund your Rates Exchange account, see [Fund & Withdraw](fund-and-withdraw.md).

Check the exact market, expiry, notional, and required funds before placing an order. The quoted rate is annualized; your fixed payment also depends on notional and the time remaining until expiry.

See the [Glossary](../glossary.md) for position terminology and [Orders & Execution](../market-mechanics/order-book.md) for order behavior.
