# Core Contributors

Rates Exchange is led by co-founders [Nico](https://x.com/nicoypei) and Y7 and built by a team of engineers, traders, and researchers with experience across Coinbase, Groq (acquired by NVIDIA), Elements (acquired by Circle), Goldman Sachs, Ondo, Chaos Labs, Bloomberg, Bain Capital Crypto, and Frax, with PhDs from Princeton and Stanford. The team is also advised by the former Head of Growth at Ethena.

The team combines expertise in derivatives trading, market structure, smart contracts, risk management, and high-performance systems.

Rates Exchange was created to bring the efficiency and precision of institutional rates markets onchain without sacrificing DeFi’s open access, transparency, or composability.

Rates Exchange cap table consists solely of the team and strategic institutional investors deploying proprietary capital, with no LP-backed venture funds. This allows the team to build with long-term focus.
